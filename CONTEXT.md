# WriteNow

WriteNow is a social writing space where writers develop entries from prompts and may publish or submit them to daily contests.

## Language

**Entry**:
A writer's saved work, including its draft or published content and descriptive metadata.
_Avoid_: Story when referring to unpublished work

**Daily contest**:
The dated competition in which writers respond to one fixed prompt and readers choose among submitted entries.
_Avoid_: Daily prompt when referring to the competition itself

**Contest draft**:
An entry being written for a particular daily contest that remains eligible for submission to that contest.
_Avoid_: Contest entry before submission

**Contest entry**:
An entry that has been submitted to a daily contest and is therefore public and immutable.
_Avoid_: Contest draft

**Prompt genre**:
The genre assigned to a writing prompt.
_Avoid_: Story genre

**Story genre**:
The canonical genre the author assigns to an entry based on the work they intend to write; `Other` may carry a more specific custom label.
_Avoid_: Prompt genre
